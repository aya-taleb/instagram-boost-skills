# Job spec: instagram-profile

Written before opening Jake's ig-profile.

**The job.** Make the profile turn a curious visitor into a follower or a customer, for one
business at a time: name field, bio, links, highlights, pinned posts, and for a local business
the address, hours and action buttons.

**Inputs.** The active business file; the current profile as the user pastes it (or a
screenshot they share); for local businesses, the local block.

**Output.** Two or three bio options inside the 150 character limit, a name field that holds a
searchable phrase (30 characters), the link plan (up to 5, first one matters most), highlight
names and order, which 3 posts to pin, and for local: address, hours, the right action button,
category. A short before/after check. Saves the chosen version to account.md.

**Good looks like.** A visitor can answer in 3 seconds: what is this, is it for me, what do I
do next. Counts are exact (a tool does the counting). No claims that aren't in the file.

**Must not.** Log in or change the profile for the user. Invent awards, follower numbers,
press. Promise that a bio will grow followers.

**Hands off to.** instagram-plan (pinned posts to make), instagram-story (highlights to fill),
instagram-audit.
