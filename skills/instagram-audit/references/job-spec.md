# Job spec: instagram-audit

Written before opening Jake's ig-audit.

**The job.** Once a month, tell the founder what is working on Instagram for one business and
what to change next month, from their own numbers.

**Inputs.** Instagram Insights numbers the user exports (Meta Business Suite CSV) or pastes,
log.md, plan.md history, the business's goals. Never scraped.

**Output.** A short read: what the numbers say by format and by theme, the top and bottom
posts and why, whether the account is reaching new people, whether it moves people to act
(profile visits, link taps, DMs, sales if known). Then keep 3, stop 3, try 3, tied to the goals.
A scorecard to compare next month. Saved to audits/<date>.md.

**Good looks like.** Uses rates, not raw totals (shares and saves per reach). Says when the
sample is too small. Ties advice to the business goal (seats sold, trial signups).

**Must not.** Scrape or log in. Invent numbers or benchmarks. Claim certainty from 6 posts.

**Hands off to.** instagram-plan (next month), instagram-profile if profile visits do not turn
into follows.
