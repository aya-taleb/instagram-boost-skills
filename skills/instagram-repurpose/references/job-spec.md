# Job spec: instagram-repurpose

Written before opening Jake's ig-repurpose.

**The job.** Turn something the user already made (blog post, newsletter, LinkedIn post,
podcast, talk, video they own, an old Instagram post) into Instagram posts for one business.

**Inputs.** The source as text or a file the user owns; for audio or video, a transcript from
transcribe (multimedia-skills); the business file; this week's plan.

**Output.** A map of what in the source is worth posting (the strongest idea, the steps, the
line people would quote, the story), then 2 to 5 Instagram pieces with format chosen per idea
(Reel, carousel, stories, single image), each a ready brief for the next skill. Saved to
repurpose/<date>-<slug>.md.

**Good looks like.** Picks ideas, not paragraphs. Each piece stands alone for someone who never
saw the source. Quotes are word for word from the source. Credits other people quoted.

**Must not.** Download media from YouTube, Instagram, TikTok or similar. Repurpose someone
else's content as the user's own. Add facts the source does not have.

**Hands off to.** instagram-carousel, ig-reel, instagram-story, talking-head-cards (for clips
with cards), instagram-plan (to slot the pieces).
