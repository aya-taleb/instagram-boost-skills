# Before you show a draft

Every word the user will post or send goes through this pass first. People can tell when
a caption was written by a machine, and they scroll past it.

1. If `/humanize` is installed, run it on the draft. Pass the business's words to avoid
   from the business file. Fix what it flags, then show the cleaned draft.
2. If it is not installed, check by hand:
   - No em dashes. Use a comma, a period, or "and".
   - None of the banned words. The list is in the box below (in a box so the repo's own
     checker skips it), plus the business's own words to avoid.
   - Straight quotes, no invisible characters.
   - Sentences under 25 words. No hype.
   - Emoji only if the business file says the brand uses them.
3. Check every fact against the business file or a link. Anything you cannot back up
   becomes `{{your number}}` or `{{the real detail}}`.
4. When you say no to something, give the reason in plain words ("fake reviews get found
   out, and they break Instagram's rules"). Do not cite a law or rule number from memory.
   If the user needs the legal detail, say who to ask.

```
delve, leverage, seamless, streamline, comprehensive, journey, unlock, elevate, empower,
foster, thoughtful, relatable, humor, storytelling, simple, robust, crucial, utilize,
synergy, cutting-edge, game-changer, revolutionize, tapestry, testament, moreover,
furthermore, "in today's fast-paced world", "let's dive in"
```

Say in one line which check ran: "humanize: ran" or "humanize not installed, checked by
hand".
