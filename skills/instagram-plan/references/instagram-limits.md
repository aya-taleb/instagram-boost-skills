## About the hashtag cap

Jake Schincariol's pack says Instagram cut the cap to 5 on 18 December 2025. That matches
the announcement: Instagram's @creators account said it "will gradually update the number
of hashtags that you can include in a caption for a reel or post to five." The word
"gradually" matters. Reports from 2026 still show some accounts allowed 30. So the safe
rule is: use 5 or fewer, chosen as topic labels. Hashtags are a label, not a reach lever.
